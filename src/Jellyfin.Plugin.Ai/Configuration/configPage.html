Advanced settings
These settings control spending in detail. The defaults are safe; check the messages above after changing them.

Limit per provider

Each provider can have its own monthly limit, as an amount or as a share of the overall limit (mix them as you like). A provider stops at whichever limit it reaches first. Local services cost nothing and have no limit.

Costs

For taxes charged on overseas services (such as GST) or your card's foreign-transaction fee, so limits match what you actually pay. Default 0.
